About CampusBazaar

A Student friendly buy-sell and exchange Bazar

University students trade constantly — used textbooks, gadgets, cycles, dorm items — but the usual channels (scattered Facebook groups) offer no way to verify who you're dealing with. CampusBazaar centralizes buying, selling, and exchanging into one platform, built specifically around trust: verified student IDs reviewed by each university's own admin, seller ratings, and listings that clear the moment they're sold.
